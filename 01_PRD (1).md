## 2. Executive Summary

**Product.** Ledgerly (working name) is a web-based, multi-tenant accounting platform that lets multiple independent businesses register, configure their organization, record sales and purchases, manage receivables and payables, and produce financial reports — all backed by a double-entry accounting engine.

**Purpose.** Replace spreadsheets, disconnected invoicing tools, and manual bookkeeping with a single system of record where every financial transaction is captured once, posted correctly to the ledger, and traceable.

**Problem solved.** Small and medium businesses commonly struggle with inconsistent invoicing, untracked receivables, manual reconciliation, weak audit trails, and difficulty preparing accurate reports and tax data. *(These are assumed pain points pending research — see Section 4.)*

**Value to businesses.**
- Faster, more consistent invoicing and payment collection.
- A trustworthy ledger: balanced entries, controlled corrections, full audit trail.
- On-demand financial reports (P&L, balance sheet, trial balance, ageing).
- Role-based collaboration between owners, accountants, staff, and external accountants.
- A foundation that can accommodate jurisdiction-specific tax rules (India GST is a candidate, not confirmed).

**Delivery approach.** This PRD proposes a deliberately narrow **MVP** focused on *correctness of the accounting core* (organization, access control, contacts, items, invoicing, payments, expenses/bills, chart of accounts, journals, core reports, audit log) rather than breadth. Advanced capabilities (bank feeds, inventory, multi-currency, automation, mobile apps) are phased after MVP.

### 5.2 Personas

#### Persona 1 — Business Owner ("Owner")
- **Goals:** Get paid on time; know profitability and cash position; stay compliant without becoming an accountant.
- **Responsibilities:** Sets up the organization, invites team members, issues or approves invoices, reviews reports.
- **Primary needs:** Fast invoicing; clear dashboard; simple onboarding; control over who can do what.
- **Likely permissions:** Full organization control.

#### Persona 2 — Accountant / Bookkeeper ("Accountant")
- **Goals:** Maintain correct books; reconcile; prepare period-end and tax data.
- **Responsibilities:** Chart of accounts, journal entries, corrections via reversal, period closing, report review.
- **Primary needs:** Reliable double-entry, controlled corrections, audit trail, trial balance, exports.
- **Likely permissions:** Accounting engine and reports; limited settings.

#### Persona 3 — Finance Manager
- **Goals:** Oversee receivables/payables, approve spend, monitor cash.
- **Responsibilities:** Approve bills/expenses (if workflow enabled), review ageing, manage vendor payments.
- **Primary needs:** Ageing reports, approval queue, visibility across transactions.

#### Persona 4 — Finance Staff / Billing Clerk ("Finance staff")
- **Goals:** Create invoices, record payments and bills accurately and quickly.
- **Responsibilities:** Day-to-day transaction entry.
- **Primary needs:** Efficient forms, validation, drafts, attachments; restrictions preventing damage to posted data.

#### Persona 5 — Employee Submitting Expenses ("Employee")
- **Goals:** Submit expense claims with receipts and get reimbursed.
- **Responsibilities:** Create expense claims; cannot see broader financials.
- **Primary needs:** Simple submission, status visibility. *(Employee expense claims are P2 unless the business confirms otherwise.)*

#### Persona 6 — Organization Administrator ("Admin")
- **Goals:** Manage users, roles, and organization settings without necessarily having full financial authority.
- **Responsibilities:** Invitations, membership, configuration.
- **Primary needs:** User management; settings; security controls.

#### Persona 7 — External Accountant
- **Goals:** Access a client's books for review, adjustments, and filing support.
- **Responsibilities:** Works across one or more client organizations under invitation.
- **Primary needs:** Organization switching; scoped, revocable access; exports; audit visibility.

#### Persona 8 — Read-only User (Auditor / Investor / Advisor)
- **Goals:** View financial data without altering it.
- **Primary needs:** Report and transaction viewing; no write access.

#### Persona 9 — Platform Administrator (internal, vendor staff)
- **Goals:** Operate the SaaS: tenants, plans, support, suspension.
- **Responsibilities:** Platform-level operations only; **must not** have default access to tenant financial data **[REC]**.
- **Primary needs:** Tenant lifecycle management, support tooling, audited impersonation if ever permitted **[TBD]**.

### 7A. Organization and Tenant Management

**Purpose.** Allow a business to register, configure its organization (tenant), and be strictly isolated from all other tenants. Allow users who belong to multiple organizations to switch between them.

**Actors.** Owner, Admin, External Accountant, Platform Administrator.

**Preconditions.** Registering user has a verified email (FR-AUTH-002). For switching: user holds an active membership in ≥2 organizations.

**Business rules.**
- BR-ORG-1: Every tenant-owned record belongs to exactly one organization.
- BR-ORG-2: A user's access is determined by (user, organization, role) membership; no cross-organization visibility by default.
- BR-ORG-3: Base currency is set at organization creation. Changing it after transactions exist is **not allowed in MVP** [REC] and requires an approved migration procedure [TBD].
- BR-ORG-4: Fiscal year start month is configurable at setup; changing it after posting requires accountant confirmation [VALIDATE-ACCT].
- BR-ORG-5: An organization must always have at least one Owner.

**Expected behavior.** Registration creates a user, an organization, and an Owner membership, seeds a default chart of accounts and tax placeholders, and lands the user on a guided setup. The active organization is explicit in every session; switching changes all data context.

**Error and edge cases.** Duplicate organization names (allowed per tenant space; uniqueness not required) [ASSUMPTION]; user attempts to remove the last Owner (blocked); organization suspended by platform (read-only or blocked — see 7K); user removed from org while active session exists (access revoked on next request); invalid currency/locale; switching to an organization the user no longer belongs to.

**Dependencies.** Authentication (B); default chart of accounts (H); notifications (L).

| ID | Requirement | MoSCoW | Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-ORG-001 | A user can register a new organization with legal/business name, country, base currency, time zone, and fiscal year start. | M | MVP |
| FR-ORG-002 | The system shall seed each new organization with a default chart of accounts appropriate to the selected setup [TBD template per market]. | M | MVP |
| FR-ORG-003 | Owners/Admins can edit organization profile: name, address, contact details, logo, tax registration identifiers, invoice defaults. | M | MVP |
| FR-ORG-004 | Base currency and fiscal year start are set at creation; edits are restricted per BR-ORG-3/4. | M | MVP |
| FR-ORG-005 | A user belonging to multiple organizations can switch the active organization without re-authenticating. | S | MVP |
| FR-ORG-006 | The system shall enforce tenant isolation on every read and write of tenant data, including reports, attachments, exports, notifications, and background jobs. | M | MVP |
| FR-ORG-007 | Organization-level settings: date format, number format, locale, default payment terms, default tax. | S | MVP |
| FR-ORG-008 | Owners can capture opening balances as of a conversion date (Trial-balance style or per-invoice/bill). | M | MVP |

**Acceptance criteria.**
- AC-ORG-1: Given Org A and Org B, a user with membership only in A cannot read, list, search, export, or reference any record of B by any route (UI, direct URL, report, attachment link, background-generated file).
- AC-ORG-2: Registration produces an organization with an Owner, default chart of accounts, and fiscal year set.
- AC-ORG-3: Attempt to remove or demote the last Owner is rejected with a clear message.
- AC-ORG-4: Changing base currency after any posted transaction is blocked.
- AC-ORG-5: Switching organizations never displays data from the previous organization.

### 7B. Authentication and Access Control

**Purpose.** Securely identify users and constrain what each can do within each organization.

**Actors.** All users; Platform Administrator.

**Preconditions.** Valid email address; for invitations, an Admin/Owner with invite permission.

**Business rules.**
- BR-AUTH-1: Authorization is evaluated server-side on every request using (user, organization, role/permission).
- BR-AUTH-2: Platform-level roles are separate from organization-level roles.
- BR-AUTH-3: Invitations are single-use, expire after a period [TBD], and bind to the invited email.
- BR-AUTH-4: Permission changes take effect without requiring the affected user to re-login [REC].
- BR-AUTH-5: Passwords are never stored or logged in recoverable form; password-reset tokens are single-use and time-limited [TBD duration].

**Expected behavior.** Standard email/password registration with verification; login with throttling; password recovery by email; configurable session lifetime; users can view and revoke sessions; Owners/Admins invite users by email with a role; invited users accept and become members.

**Error and edge cases.** Invitation sent to an email already registered (attach membership); invitation expired/revoked/already used; invited email differs from signed-in email; repeated failed logins (rate limit/lockout [TBD thresholds]); password reset for unknown email (generic response to avoid enumeration); removing a member with in-progress drafts (drafts retained, attributed to former user); user deactivated mid-session.

**Dependencies.** Email provider (Section 12); organization module (A).

## 8. Core User Journeys

Each journey lists the main path, alternate paths (**ALT**), and material failure scenarios (**FAIL**).

### 8.1 Registering a Business

**Actor:** Prospective Owner.

1. User opens the sign-up page and enters name, email, and password.
2. System creates an unverified account and sends a verification email.
3. User verifies email via the link.
4. User is guided through organization setup: business name, country, base currency, time zone, fiscal year start, tax registration (optional at this step).
5. System creates the organization, an Owner membership, a default chart of accounts, and default settings.
6. User lands on the dashboard with setup checklist (add customers/items, invite team, enter opening balances, issue first invoice).

- **ALT-1:** User already has an account → logs in and chooses "Create organization".
- **ALT-2:** User received an invitation instead → follows invitation flow (8.2).
- **ALT-3:** User abandons mid-setup → returns later; setup resumes from last completed step.
- **FAIL-1:** Email already registered → generic guidance to log in/reset.
- **FAIL-2:** Verification link expired → resend option.
- **FAIL-3:** Weak password or invalid fields → inline validation messages.
- **FAIL-4:** Organization creation partially fails → no half-created tenant remains; user can retry.

### 8.2 Inviting a Team Member

**Actors:** Owner/Admin (inviter); invitee.

1. Inviter opens Users & Roles, enters invitee email and selects a role.
2. System creates a pending invitation and emails a single-use link.
3. Invitee opens the link; if new, registers and verifies; if existing, signs in.
4. Invitee accepts; membership becomes active with the chosen role.
5. Audit log records the invitation, acceptance, and role.

- **ALT-1:** Inviter resends or revokes a pending invitation.
- **ALT-2:** Invitee is an external accountant already holding other memberships → org appears in their organization switcher.
- **FAIL-1:** Link expired/revoked/used → clear message; inviter can resend.
- **FAIL-2:** Invitee signs in with a different email than invited → blocked with explanation.
- **FAIL-3:** Inviter lacks permission or organization plan user-limit reached [TBD] → blocked with explanation.

### 8.3 Creating a Customer

**Actors:** Finance staff / Owner / Accountant.

1. User selects New Customer; enters name, contact details, billing address, tax identifier (if applicable), payment terms.
2. System validates required fields and warns on potential duplicates.
3. User saves; customer appears in the list and can be chosen on invoices.

- **ALT-1:** Create a customer inline while drafting an invoice.
- **ALT-2:** User overrides the duplicate warning intentionally.
- **FAIL-1:** Invalid email or tax identifier format → inline error.
- **FAIL-2:** User lacks permission → action unavailable; direct request rejected.

### 8.4 Creating and Issuing an Invoice

**Actors:** Finance staff / Owner / Accountant; customer (recipient).

1. User selects New Invoice and chooses a customer; defaults (terms, currency, tax treatment) are applied.
2. User adds line items (from catalog or free-text), quantities, rates, discounts, taxes.
3. System shows live totals per approved calculation rules; autosaves as Draft.
4. User reviews preview and chooses **Issue**.
5. System, in one atomic operation: validates period is open and data complete; assigns the next invoice number; snapshots customer/item/tax data; posts the journal; sets status Issued; writes audit entries.
6. User chooses Send; system generates PDF and emails the customer; delivery status is recorded.
7. Invoice appears in receivables; overdue status derives from the due date.

- **ALT-1:** Save draft and return later; draft has no accounting effect.
- **ALT-2:** Issue without sending; send later or download PDF.
- **ALT-3:** Clone a previous invoice.
- **ALT-4:** Issued invoice contains an error → void (if no payments) and reissue, or credit note [TBD].
- **FAIL-1:** Period locked → issue rejected with message and lock date.
- **FAIL-2:** Double-click / network retry → one invoice, one number, one journal.
- **FAIL-3:** Archived customer/item or missing account mapping → blocked with actionable message.
- **FAIL-4:** Email send fails → invoice remains issued; delivery shows failed with retry.
- **FAIL-5:** Posting fails → invoice remains Draft; no number consumed [TBD policy], no partial journal.

### 8.5 Recording a Full or Partial Payment

**Actors:** Finance staff / Accountant / Owner.

1. User opens the invoice (or customer) and selects Record Payment.
2. User enters amount, date, method, deposit account, reference.
3. System proposes allocation across open invoices (default behavior [TBD]); user adjusts.
4. System validates allocations (≤ payment amount, ≤ each invoice's outstanding balance).
5. System, atomically, posts the payment journal (Dr Bank/Cash, Cr AR), stores allocations, updates invoice balances/status, audits.
6. Optional: send a payment receipt.

- **ALT-1 (partial):** Amount < invoice total → invoice becomes Partially Paid; outstanding shown.
- **ALT-2 (multi-invoice):** One payment split across several invoices.
- **ALT-3 (overpayment):** Remainder stored as customer credit or blocked [TBD].
- **ALT-4:** Payment recorded in error → reversed, restoring balances.
- **FAIL-1:** Over-allocation → rejected.
- **FAIL-2:** Payment date in locked period → rejected.
- **FAIL-3:** Concurrent payments racing for the same balance → at most the valid total is accepted; the other receives a clear conflict message.
- **FAIL-4:** Duplicate submission → single payment.

### 8.6 Recording an Expense or Vendor Bill

**Actors:** Finance staff / Accountant / Owner (Employee for claims in P2).

*Expense (paid immediately):*
1. User selects New Expense; enters date, category, amount, tax, paid-from account, vendor (optional).
2. User attaches receipt.
3. System posts Dr Expense / Cr Bank-Cash-Card and audits.

*Vendor bill (payable):*
1. User selects New Bill; chooses vendor, enters bill number, dates, lines, taxes; attaches the bill document.
2. User saves and posts; system posts Dr Expense/Input tax, Cr AP; status Open.
3. Later, user records a bill payment (full or partial) with allocation, posting Dr AP / Cr Bank-Cash.

- **ALT-1:** Save bill as draft.
- **ALT-2:** If approvals are enabled (P2), submission routes to an approver.
- **ALT-3:** Void a mistaken bill (no payments).
- **FAIL-1:** Duplicate vendor bill number → warning/block.
- **FAIL-2:** Locked period → rejected.
- **FAIL-3:** Attachment too large or disallowed type → rejected with limits displayed.

### 8.7 Reviewing Financial Reports

**Actors:** Owner / Accountant / Finance Manager / Read-only / External Accountant.

1. User opens Reports and selects a report (e.g., P&L).
2. User sets date range / as-of date and filters.
3. System displays results with totals; user drills down to ledger and source documents.
4. User exports (CSV/PDF) as needed.

- **ALT-1:** Compare with prior period.
- **ALT-2:** Accountant cross-checks Trial Balance, then GL, then source documents.
- **FAIL-1:** No data → empty state with guidance.
- **FAIL-2:** Large report → progress indicator or asynchronous export [TBD].
- **FAIL-3:** User lacks report permission → report hidden/denied.

### 8.8 Managing Organization Settings

**Actors:** Owner / Admin (some settings Accountant).

1. User opens Settings and selects a section: Profile, Currency & Fiscal Year, Taxes, Invoice Preferences, Numbering, Users & Roles, Chart of Accounts, Lock Date, Data Export.
2. User edits and saves; system validates, applies to future transactions only (unless specified), and audits the change.

- **ALT-1:** Setting a lock date → blocks postings into locked periods.
- **ALT-2:** Changing invoice number format → affects only future issued invoices; sequence continuity retained.
- **FAIL-1:** Restricted change (e.g., base currency after postings) → blocked with explanation.
- **FAIL-2:** Insufficient permission → setting read-only or hidden.
- **FAIL-3:** Setting a lock date that would conflict with open drafts/in-flight items → warning and resolution path.

### 9.5 Invoice State Transitions

```
                +----------+   issue    +---------+  allocate(partial)  +----------------+
   create ----> |  Draft   | ---------> | Issued  | ------------------> | Partially Paid |
                +----------+            +---------+                     +----------------+
                  |    ^                  |   |                              |      |
          delete  |    | (edit)           |   | allocate(full)               |      | allocate(rest)
                  v    |                  |   v                              |      v
              (removed)                   |  +------+  <---------------------+   (Paid)
                                          |  | Paid |
                                  void*   |  +------+
                                          v     |  unapply/reverse payment → back to Issued/Partially Paid
                                       +------+
                                       | Void |  (terminal)
                                       +------+
  *Void allowed only when no payments/credits are applied and period is open.
  "Overdue" is derived (due date passed AND outstanding > 0), not a stored state.
```

| From | To | Condition | Accounting effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft | Issued | Valid data, open period, number assigned | Post AR/Revenue/Tax journal |
| Draft | (Deleted) | Any time | None |
| Issued | Partially Paid | Payment allocated, 0 < allocated < total | Payment journal |
| Issued/Partially Paid | Paid | Allocated = total | Payment journal |
| Paid/Partially Paid | Issued/Partially Paid | Payment reversed | Reversing payment journal |
| Issued | Void | No allocations; open period; reason recorded | Reversing journal |
| Issued/Partially Paid/Paid | Adjusted via Credit Note | Credit note issued [TBD] | Reversing/adjusting journal |

Prohibited: Void → any other state; editing financial fields of Issued or later states; deleting Issued or later invoices.

### 9.6 Payment Allocation Rules

- BR-ALLOC-1: Σ allocations of a payment ≤ payment amount; excess is unallocated credit (policy **TBD**).
- BR-ALLOC-2: Allocation to an invoice ≤ its outstanding balance at the time of posting (checked with concurrency protection).
- BR-ALLOC-3: Allocations only to invoices of the same customer (and same currency in P2).
- BR-ALLOC-4: Allocations cannot target Draft or Void invoices.
- BR-ALLOC-5: Default allocation proposal (e.g., oldest-first) is a UX default, not an accounting rule **[TBD]**.
- BR-ALLOC-6: Reversing a payment reverses all its allocations atomically.
- BR-ALLOC-7: Outstanding balance = invoice total − Σ allocations − Σ applied credits.
- BR-ALLOC-8: The same rules apply symmetrically to vendor bill payments.

### 9.7 Double-Entry Integrity

- BR-DE-1: Every journal has ≥ 2 lines; Σ debits = Σ credits exactly at stored precision.
- BR-DE-2: Each line has either a debit or a credit amount ≥ 0 (not both); zero-amount lines are discouraged and policy **TBD**.
- BR-DE-3: Journals can post only to active, postable accounts of the same organization.
- BR-DE-4: Balance is enforced in the accounting engine itself, independent of any UI or API entry route.
- BR-DE-5: Account balances and reports derive from journal lines; no independent stored balance may disagree with them (any cached/materialized balance must be reconcilable and rebuildable).
- BR-DE-6: A source document and its journal(s) are created in the same atomic transaction.

### 9.8 Preventing Unauthorized Financial Changes

- BR-SEC-1: Financial mutations require explicit permissions per action (create, issue, void, reverse, lock/unlock, post manual journal).
- BR-SEC-2: Separation of duties: configurable so that creators cannot approve their own items (P2 workflows) [REC].
- BR-SEC-3: Lock-date changes and un-locking require elevated roles and are audited with reason.
- BR-SEC-4: Direct modification of posted data through any back-office tool is prohibited; any exceptional operational correction requires an audited, approved procedure **[TBD]**.
- BR-SEC-5: Platform administrators cannot alter tenant financial records.

### 9.9 Reversals and Corrections of Posted Transactions

- BR-REV-1: Posted transactions are never edited or deleted in place.
- BR-REV-2: Correction methods: **void** (full reversal of a document), **reverse journal** (mirror entry), **credit note/vendor credit** (document-based adjustment), **adjusting journal** (manual).
- BR-REV-3: A reversal references the original, carries a reason, and cannot itself be reversed again (instead create a new correct entry).
- BR-REV-4: Reversal date defaults to the original date if the period is open, otherwise to the first open date, subject to accountant policy **[VALIDATE-ACCT]**.
- BR-REV-5: Voiding/reversal updates derived balances, statuses, and reports consistently in the same transaction.

### 9.10 Audit Trail Requirements

- BR-AUD-1: Log: who, when, organization, action, object type/ID, before/after (for key financial fields), source (UI/API/system), correlation/request ID.
- BR-AUD-2: Audit log is append-only; not modifiable by tenant roles or platform admins.
- BR-AUD-3: Required events: create/issue/void/reverse/post financial documents, payment recording and reversal, manual journals, lock/unlock, tax/number/currency settings changes, role/membership changes, login and security events, data exports, platform admin access.
- BR-AUD-4: Failure to write an audit record for a financial action aborts that action.
- BR-AUD-5: Retention and review period of audit logs: **TBD** [VALIDATE-ACCT].

### 9.11 Transaction Consistency and Duplicate Request Prevention

- BR-TXN-1: Each financial operation (issue, pay, void, reverse, post) is **atomic**: it either fully succeeds or leaves no financial trace.
- BR-TXN-2: Mutating financial requests carry an idempotency key (or equivalent) so network retries and double-clicks do not duplicate effects.
- BR-TXN-3: Concurrent operations on the same document are serialized or optimistically controlled; stale updates are rejected with a clear message.
- BR-TXN-4: Sequence assignment, balance checks, and allocation limits are enforced with concurrency-safe mechanisms.
- BR-TXN-5: Background jobs (PDF/email) are retryable and idempotent, and their failure never corrupts financial state.
- BR-TXN-6: Recovery behavior after partial infrastructure failure (e.g., storage or email provider outage) is defined: financial posting is not blocked by non-critical side effects.

### 9.12 Items Requiring Accountant / Stakeholder Validation

See Section 15 and the closing summary for the consolidated list: rounding policy, tax order, gapless numbering, period-end/year-end closing, cash-flow method, cash vs. accrual basis, FX treatment, credit note and refund accounting, retention periods, jurisdictional invoice content.

## 13. UX and Product Experience Requirements

> Zoho Books is a **functional reference** for category expectations (dashboard, invoicing, banking, reports; publicly documented — Appendix A). This product should have an **original, consistent interface** and its own visual identity. No copying of Zoho's layouts, text, icons, or branding.

### 13.1 Design Principles [REC]
1. **Clarity for non-accountants, depth for accountants.** Plain-language primary flows; advanced ledger views available but not in the way.
2. **Safe by design.** Irreversible actions are explicit; corrections are guided.
3. **Speed for repeat tasks.** Keyboard-friendly entry, sensible defaults, drafts, autosave.
4. **Consistency.** One design system; same patterns for lists, forms, and detail pages.
5. **Trust through transparency.** Show how numbers are derived; drill down to source.

### 13.2 Dashboard
- Overview widgets (candidates): total receivables (current/overdue), total payables, cash position (when bank accounts exist), income vs. expense for a selectable period, top expenses, overdue invoices list, recent activity, setup checklist for new organizations.
- All figures derive from posted data and reconcile to reports; each widget links to the relevant report or list.
- Date-range control with clear label of the period shown.
- Widget set and customization: basic in MVP; personalization is P2.

### 13.3 Navigation
- Persistent primary navigation grouped by domain: Dashboard, Sales (Customers, Invoices, Payments), Purchases (Vendors, Bills, Expenses), Accounting (Chart of Accounts, Journals, Lock Date), Reports, Settings.
- Organization switcher with clear indication of the active organization at all times.
- Global search for contacts, invoices, bills (scope [TBD]).
- Breadcrumbs or clear back-navigation on detail views.
- Navigation items hidden or disabled according to permissions, with explanatory messages where appropriate.

### 13.4 Data Tables and Filters
- Server-side pagination, sorting, and filtering; consistent column controls.
- Status chips combine color with text/icon.
- Saved/default filters (e.g., "Overdue") — basic in MVP.
- Bulk actions limited to safe operations in MVP (e.g., export, archive); no bulk financial posting in MVP.
- Responsive tables degrade gracefully (priority columns, horizontal scroll with fixed identifier column, or card view).
- Export respects current filters.

### 13.5 Forms and Validation
- Inline, field-level validation with specific, actionable messages; summary of errors at submit.
- Required fields clearly indicated; sensible defaults; unsaved-changes warning.
- Input formats follow organization locale (dates, numbers, currency).
- Prevent double submission (disabled state + idempotency).
- Concurrency/conflict messages explain what changed and offer reload.
- Destructive/irreversible confirmations explain accounting consequences (e.g., "Issuing will assign invoice number and lock editing").

### 13.6 Invoice Creation Experience
- Single-page editor with customer selection (with inline create), dates and terms, line-item grid, totals panel, notes/terms, attachments.
- Keyboard-friendly line entry; item search/autocomplete; reorder/remove lines.
- Live totals including per-rate tax breakdown, with rounding behavior consistent with the issued result.
- Clear **Draft** indicator; autosave state shown; preview matches PDF.
- Primary actions: Save Draft, Issue, Issue & Send; Issue explains it becomes locked and numbered.
- Post-issue view: status, balance, payments, activity history, send/download, record payment, void/credit note (permission-gated).
- Warnings (not blockers) for unusual input: future date, zero total, missing tax on taxable item [TBD].

### 13.7 Empty States
- Each list/report has a helpful empty state: what it is, why it is empty, and the next action (e.g., "Create your first customer").
- Setup checklist for new organizations; empty reports indicate selected filters/period.

### 13.8 Loading States
- Skeletons or progress indicators for tables, reports, and PDF generation.
- Long-running operations (large exports/reports) show progress and allow leaving the page, notifying on completion [TBD].
- No misleading zero values while data loads.

### 13.9 Error Messages
- Plain language, state what happened, what the user can do next, and avoid exposing technical internals.
- Distinguish validation errors, permission errors, conflict/concurrency errors, period-lock errors, and system errors.
- Lock-period errors name the lock date; permission errors suggest contacting an Owner/Admin.
- Support reference/correlation ID shown for unexpected errors.

### 13.10 Mobile and Responsive Layouts
- MVP: fully **responsive web** supporting common phone, tablet, and desktop widths; native apps are Future.
- Priority mobile tasks: view dashboard, view/send invoices, record payments, capture expenses with receipt photo, approve items (P2).
- Complex tasks (journals, chart of accounts, wide reports) remain usable via responsive adaptation, though full-featured desktop usage is acceptable.
- Touch targets and spacing meet accessibility guidance.

### 13.11 Accessibility
- Conform to the NFR-ACC targets: keyboard navigation, screen-reader semantics, contrast, focus visibility, accessible tables and form errors.
- PDF documents should be legible and, where feasible, accessible [TBD].
- Accessibility acceptance included in the Definition of Done for each UI feature.

### 13.12 Localization and Formatting
- Locale-aware dates, numbers, currencies; right-to-left layout and translations **TBD** by target market.
- Organization-level formats override user defaults for documents so printed documents are consistent.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria and Definition of Done

### 14.1 Definition of Done (per requirement/feature)
A requirement is **Done** only when all of the following hold:
1. Behavior matches the requirement and acceptance criteria, including listed edge/error cases.
2. Automated tests (unit, integration, and where applicable end-to-end) are written and passing; Must Have requirements have no untested acceptance criteria.
3. Authorization and tenant-isolation tests exist for any new endpoint/resource.
4. Financial operations are atomic, idempotent where required, and audited.
5. Accessibility checks pass for UI work.
6. Code reviewed and merged per team standards; no unresolved critical/high defects.
7. Documentation updated (user-facing help and internal docs).
8. Observability: relevant logs/metrics/alerts in place.
9. QA sign-off; for financial logic, accountant sign-off on test cases.
10. Demonstrated in a staging environment.

### 14.2 Release-Level Acceptance Criteria — **Testable Now**
*(These do not depend on pending business decisions; they can be written as test cases immediately.)*

| ID | Scenario | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| REL-001 | **Tenant isolation** | A user in Org A cannot read, list, search, export, download an attachment of, or modify any record of Org B through UI, API, direct object URL, report, or generated file. |
| REL-002 | **Balanced postings** | For every posted journal in all test datasets, Σ debits = Σ credits. An unbalanced entry is rejected in every entry channel. |
| REL-003 | **Immutability** | Posted invoices, bills, payments, and journals cannot be edited or deleted; corrections require void/reversal and create new linked entries. |
| REL-004 | **Partial payments** | A payment less than the invoice total leaves the invoice Partially Paid with the correct outstanding balance; AR ageing and customer balance agree. |
| REL-005 | **Over-allocation prevention** | Allocation exceeding the invoice outstanding balance (including under concurrent submissions) is rejected. |
| REL-006 | **Authorization** | Users without permission cannot perform restricted actions (issue, void, reverse, lock/unlock, manage users, post journals) via UI or direct request; Read-only users cannot mutate anything. |
| REL-007 | **Atomicity** | Injected failure at any step of issue/payment/void/bill posting leaves no partial document, journal, number consumption anomaly, or balance change. |
| REL-008 | **Audit** | Every posting, void, reversal, lock-date change, role change, and security event creates an immutable audit record with actor and timestamp. |
| REL-009 | **Duplicate requests** | Re-submitting the same issue/payment request does not create duplicate numbers, journals, or payments. |
| REL-010 | **Invoice numbering** | Concurrent issuance never produces duplicate invoice numbers within an organization. |
| REL-011 | **Period lock** | Posting, voiding, or reversing into a locked period is rejected. |
| REL-012 | **Ledger-report consistency** | Trial balance balances; Balance Sheet balances; AR/AP ageing totals equal control account balances; exports equal on-screen figures. |
| REL-013 | **Snapshot integrity** | Editing customer/item/tax settings does not change previously issued documents or their PDFs. |
| REL-014 | **Invitation security** | Expired, revoked, or reused invitations cannot be accepted; invitation is bound to the invited email. |
| REL-015 | **Authentication hardening** | Login throttling works; reset links are single-use and time-limited; account existence is not disclosed. |
| REL-016 | **Attachment access** | Attachments cannot be fetched without authorization for the owning organization. |
| REL-017 | **Platform admin boundary** | Platform administrators cannot view or alter tenant financial data outside the defined audited support flow. |
| REL-018 | **Suspension/reactivation** | Suspending an organization never deletes data; reactivation restores access (behavior while suspended per approved rule). |
